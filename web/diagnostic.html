Hardware Dog — a local-first hardware diagnostic tool in your browser

Sniff the hardware. Not the human. No account. No subscription. No tracking. Sessions stay on this machine; you choose what to export.

Camera Sniffer: hidden camera clues, without a cloud app

Looks for clues of cameras on a network you choose (through dogd), over Bluetooth and USB, and guides a physical inspection object by object. It says what it observes; a scan with no clue does not prove a place has no camera.

Bluetooth Lab: a Bluetooth LE scanner in the browser

Scan, inspect and read Bluetooth LE devices (GATT), through Web Bluetooth where the browser offers it or dogd on Linux. The source is public.

Flipper Lab: Flipper Zero over Web Serial, no app to install

Read a Flipper Zero's storage and captures over USB, from a Chromium-based browser on a computer.

USB, network, serial

A board's serial console in the browser; this computer's network and USB devices through dogd, the optional local daemon.

Limits: Web Serial and Web Bluetooth need a Chromium-based browser on a computer; network discovery needs dogd running on this computer; the Hardware Dog probe is still in development. A demo works in any browser.
