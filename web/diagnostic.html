Hardware Dog — Camera Sniffer & hardware diagnostics

Local-first diagnostic tools that run in your browser. No account. No subscription. Sessions stay on this machine; you choose what to export.

  • Camera Sniffer: clues of cameras on a network you choose (through dogd), over Bluetooth and USB, and a guided physical inspection. It says what it observes; a scan with no clue does not prove a place has no camera.
  • Bluetooth Lab: scan, inspect and read Bluetooth LE devices, through Web Bluetooth where the browser offers it or dogd on Linux.
  • Flipper Lab: read a Flipper Zero's storage and captures over USB.
  • USB, network, serial: a board's serial console in the browser; this computer's network and USB devices through dogd, the optional local daemon.

Limits: Web Serial and Web Bluetooth need a Chromium-based browser on a computer; network discovery needs dogd running on this computer; the Hardware Dog probe is still in development. A demo works in any browser.
